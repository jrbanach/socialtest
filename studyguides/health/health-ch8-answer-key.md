## VOCAB (11 terms)

1. **Microbe** — A living thing that is too small to be seen without a microscope. _(R5, D46)_
2. **Risk Factor** — Anything that increases the chance of loss or harm. _(R7, D64)_
3. **Virus** — A very tiny particle that makes copies of itself inside a cell. _(R9, D46)_
4. **Bacteria** — The smallest living microbes that can reproduce on their own. _(R1, D46)_
5. **Fungus** — A diseased, spongy growth on the body. _(R4, D46)_
6. **Protozoa** — Simple one-celled microbes that are much larger than bacteria. _(R7, D46)_
7. **White Blood Cells** — Blood cells that surround and destroy pathogens. _(R9, D53)_
8. **Antibody** — A substance in blood that helps fight pathogens. _(R1, D53)_
9. **Immunization** — Protection from getting a particular disease. _(R5, D53)_
10. **Symptom** — A change in your body's condition or function that may signify disease. _(R9, D57)_
11. **Chronic Disease** — A condition that lasts a long time or keeps coming back. _(R2, D61)_

## MULTIPLE CHOICE (11 questions)

The single "3 stages of disease in order" question has been split into 3 separate questions (one per stage), per the user's request that the 3 stages "need to be known and described independently."

**1. (D65) What can you do to protect your heart from stress?**
- ✅ Talk with an adult if you feel stress
- ❌ Eat more salt every day
- ❌ Avoid all physical activity
- ❌ Smoke to help you relax

**2. (D49) Pathogens can enter your body through cuts in your skin. What is the best way to keep germs from spreading?**
- ✅ Wash your hands often with soap and water
- ❌ Touch your eyes, nose, and mouth often
- ❌ Share cups and eating utensils with friends
- ❌ Skip the flu vaccine

**3. (D63) Which of these is a symptom of a heart attack?**
- ✅ Chest pain or pressure, sweating, and difficulty breathing
- ❌ A rash that looks like a bull's-eye on a target
- ❌ A high fever with itchy spots all over
- ❌ A sore throat with white patches

**4. (D51) What is the Incubation period of a disease?**
- ✅ The period from when a pathogen gets into your body until you start showing signs of the disease
- ❌ The time during which you show symptoms of the disease
- ❌ The time when most symptoms have gone away but you are not yet well
- ❌ The time when a disease has been completely cured

**5. (D51) What is the Acute period of a disease?**
- ✅ The time during which you show symptoms of the disease
- ❌ The period before any symptoms appear
- ❌ The time when symptoms are going away but you are not yet well
- ❌ The time when the disease lasts a long time and keeps coming back

**6. (D51) What is the Recovery period of a disease?**
- ✅ The time during which most of the symptoms go away, but you are not yet well
- ❌ The period before any symptoms appear
- ❌ The time when symptoms are at their strongest
- ❌ The time when a pathogen first enters your body

**7. (D49) Which behavior helps reduce your risk of getting a communicable disease?**
- ✅ Don't share cups, eating utensils, or bottles
- ❌ Touch your eyes, nose, and mouth often
- ❌ Sneeze without covering your mouth
- ❌ Drink from someone else's water bottle

**8. (D75) What do animal dander and nuts have in common?**
- ✅ They are both common allergens that can cause allergies
- ❌ They are both communicable diseases
- ❌ They are both caused by viruses
- ❌ They both protect your heart

**9. (D57) What disease can ticks carry?**
- ✅ Lyme disease
- ❌ Strep throat
- ❌ Chicken pox
- ❌ The flu

**10. (D58) What can untreated strep throat cause?**
- ✅ Permanent heart damage
- ❌ A common cold
- ❌ Lyme disease
- ❌ Athlete's foot

**11. (D46) Which of these diseases is caused by a virus?**
- ✅ The flu
- ❌ Strep throat (caused by bacteria)
- ❌ Athlete's foot (caused by a fungus)
- ❌ Malaria (caused by protozoa)
